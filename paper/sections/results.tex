\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}
\subsection{Task success}
Sampling with verification has the highest macro SR on both models: 81.13\% on Mistral and 86.18\% on Llama (Table~\ref{tab:effectiveness}). GoT follows at 70.62\% and 83.26\%, then Reflexion at 45.75\% and 53.12\%.

Backbone effects vary by method. Llama exceeds Mistral by 12.64 percentage points for GoT and 17.78 for ICL, but trails by 7.88 for s1 budget forcing. Sampling leads all ten Mistral tasks. On Llama, GoT leads on BDPQ and BHMQ and ties Sampling on HMPQ; its BHMQ advantage is one input out of 118.

\subsection{Output selection}
Beam's first-20 coverage is 83.38\% and 80.76\%, versus 35.74\% and 33.03\% for its rank-zero output. For Sampling, returning the first candidate from the same 40-proposal calls gives 32.36\% and 28.04\%. Verification recovers successful later candidates for 1,872 and 2,275 inputs, increasing macro SR by 48.76 and 58.14 points. These comparisons hold generation fixed and change only output selection (Appendix~\ref{app:readouts}).

\subsection{Generation workload}
Vote has the largest recorded generation workload: 400 proposals per input (Table~\ref{tab:workload}). On Mistral it produces 26,423 output tokens, compared with 2,596 for Sampling, while achieving lower SR. Reflexion's early stopping reduces mean actor trials to 2.37 and 2.27. ToT uses 20 molecular proposals and ten judging responses.

\subsection{Molecular quality}
GoT leads on Threshold SR at 41.27\% and 62.13\%, compared with Sampling's 19.83\% and 25.60\% (Table~\ref{tab:quality}). Sampling returns the first directional improvement; GoT uses improvement magnitude in search and selection.

GoT's validity is 100\% on both models, compared with 75.47\% and 50.97\% for ToT. Among each method's successful outputs, GoT has higher Stabilized RI (1.691/2.223 versus 0.947/1.153 for Sampling), lower similarity (0.393/0.257 versus 0.561/0.453), and lower SAS.

Appendix~\ref{app:reference_difficulty} reports success within fixed Mistral reference groups.
