\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}
\gellmo{} studies instruction-conditioned molecular optimization and generalization across property combinations \citep{gellmo2025}. We use its models and evaluation pipeline to compare inference strategies.

The benchmark spans beam search \citep{beam2014}, repeated sampling \citep{repeatedsampling2024}, voting \citep{voting2024}, feedback-based refinement \citep{selfrefine2023,reflexion2023}, and structured search \citep{tot2023,got2024}. We also include s1's budget-forcing component \citep{s1_2025} and few-shot prompting \citep{brown2020}. Their molecular adaptations specify candidate identity, property feedback, state scoring, and output selection under a shared final-output evaluation.
