\section{Conclusion}
We benchmark nine molecular inference strategies on two frozen models under a common single-output contract. Separate analyses of success, generation workload, and molecular quality show that candidate coverage can exceed terminal success, and directional success need not imply large improvements. Output selection, feedback access, and metric definitions are therefore essential to interpreting molecular TTS. These results characterize declared adaptations and recorded configurations, providing a basis for controlled studies of budgets, feedback, and seed variability rather than a universal method ordering.
