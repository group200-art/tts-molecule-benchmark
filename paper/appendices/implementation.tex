\section{Implementation and Evaluation Details}
\label{app:implementation}

\subsection{Checkpoints and records}
The GeLLMO-P6 adapter revisions are \nolinkurl{a6437ea4aedd75584bd9d30001c63e9808489531} (Mistral) and \nolinkurl{6a5b6ab53b8e6d3c2016370a980d57c39c749592} (Llama). Base-model upstream revision fields were unrecorded; checksums identify the local model files and source manifests. Inference uses bfloat16 base weights and unmerged PEFT adapters with adapter dtype autocasting, without quantization or offloading.

Evaluation records are keyed by task--input pair; a source molecule may appear in multiple tasks. They retain raw generations, original proposal or trajectory positions, selection provenance, and property-evaluation status. Cache reuse requires matching the input, checkpoint, generation configuration, and trajectory position.

\subsection{Property evaluation}
BBBP, HIA, and mutagenicity use ADMET-AI \citep{admet2024} columns \texttt{BBB\_Martins}, \texttt{HIA\_Hou}, and \texttt{AMES}. DRD2, penalized logP, QED, and SAS use the retained GeLLMO pipeline.

Source values retain manifest precision. Candidate comparison values pass through pandas 1.0.5/NumPy 1.18.5 \texttt{DataFrame.round(2)}, CSV serialization, and reading, with no further rounding in the metric kernel. SR uses strict improvement without an added tolerance or similarity constraint.

Accepted molecular failures include RDKit parse failures across evaluation environments and downstream kekulization failures. Unexpected missing records, corrupt identities, and infrastructure errors require resolution before aggregation. Failed outputs are retained without replacement.

\subsection{Decoding and selection}

\paragraph{Beam search.}
Decoding is deterministic, with width 20, 20 returned sequences, and a 100-new-token cap. Rank~0 is retained even when invalid.

\paragraph{Repeated Sampling.}
The 40 proposals use temperature 1, top-$p$ 0.95, top-$k$ 50, and a 100-new-token cap. The selector in Section~\ref{sec:methods} uses original positions 0--19. Logical verifier queries stop at the first pass or after 20 checks; all 40 proposals contribute to generation workload.

\paragraph{Self-Refine.}
The recorded seed is 51001. Initial and revised answers have a 250-new-token cap; feedback has a 400-token cap. Sampling uses temperature 0.7, top-$p$ 1, and top-$k$ 0. The saved prefix contains four responses: initial answer, initial feedback, first revision, and feedback on that revision. The first revision supplies the returned molecule.

\paragraph{Tree of Thoughts.}
\label{app:tot-scope}
Five voting responses select three of five initial candidates. Each selected parent generates five children; five further votes select one of the 15 children. The generator checkpoint serves as judge, with candidate properties, validity labels, and success labels omitted from voting prompts. Malformed votes contribute zero; ties follow frontier order. Children have a 160-token cap and judges a 128-token cap, with temperature 1, top-$p$ 1, and top-$k$ 0.

\paragraph{Graph of Thoughts.}
The 12 initial nodes are scored to retain six parents. Using these parents and the source molecule, the model generates six single-parent refinements, six two-parent merges, and four four-parent aggregations. Multi-parent prompts combine molecular design proposals.

Our molecular scorer assigns each evaluable node
\begin{align}
 s(y)={}&1+\frac{3}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}z_j(y)
       +0.50z_{\mathrm{sim}}(y)\nonumber\\
       &+0.25z_{\mathrm{SAS}}(y)-0.25D(y),
\end{align}
where $m$ is the number of targets, $z_j$ is min--max normalized directional improvement, $z_{\mathrm{sim}}$ is normalized source similarity, $z_{\mathrm{SAS}}$ rewards lower SAS, and $D$ flags duplicate canonical identity. Normalization is performed separately on the initial pool and the completed 28-node graph. Constant finite features receive 0.5; empty and invalid nodes receive $-200$ and $-100$. Ties preserve creation order. The scorer selects parents and the final rank-zero node; parent prompts include initial-stage feedback.

Decoding uses temperature 1, top-$p$ 1, and top-$k$ 0, with token caps of 200 for initial nodes and 220 for later nodes. Internal similarity uses unrounded Tanimoto scores on binary Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, 2,048 bits, no chirality); target properties and SAS use the retained comparison precision.

\paragraph{s1 budget forcing.}
Greedy reasoning has a 512-generated-token allowance. If EOS occurs before this budget is exhausted, the method inserts at most one literal \texttt{Wait} continuation. A separate final-answer cue allows 256 additional tokens. The parser reads only the newly generated final response.

\paragraph{Vote.}
The 400 proposals are generated in 20 calls of 20 sequences, at temperature 0.1, top-$p$ 1, top-$k$ 0, and a 256-new-token cap. Canonical isomeric SMILES use RDKit 2024.03.6. Invalid and empty response classes remain eligible to win under the identity-based rule in Section~\ref{sec:methods}.

\paragraph{Four-shot ICL.}
Four demonstrations are drawn deterministically at seed 2020 from the training pool, retaining their task labels. Filtering excludes the five held-out property combinations and demonstrations whose source or target matches any evaluation source by raw or canonical isomeric SMILES. Invalid, empty, duplicate, and unchanged source--target examples are also excluded. Decoding uses four beams, four returned sequences, length penalty 0.6, and a 100-new-token cap.

\paragraph{Reflexion.}
Actor generation uses greedy decoding with a 250-new-token cap. Each trial generates a thought and molecular action. The evaluator queries molecular properties and exposes only success and invalid-output flags to the actor. Failed nonterminal trials generate a reflection for the next trial; the final action is returned.
