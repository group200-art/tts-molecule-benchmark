\begin{abstract}
We benchmark nine test-time inference strategies on two frozen GeLLMO models for multi-property molecular optimization. Each configuration covers ten tasks and all 3,905 task--input pairs, returning one molecule under a common evaluator. We report task success, generation workload, and molecular quality. Sampling with verification achieves macro success rates of 81.13\% and 86.18\%; selecting the first candidate from the same generation calls yields 32.36\% and 28.04\%. Graph of Thoughts has lower directional success but higher success under minimum-improvement thresholds.
\end{abstract}
